# Customer Segment Profiles

This file contains the analysis of customer segments generated using clustering algorithms.

---

## Segment 0: Premium Customers
### Characteristics:
- High income
- High spending behavior
- Low churn probability

### Business Strategy:
- Offer loyalty rewards
- Provide premium memberships
- Personalized offers for retention

---

## Segment 1: Budget Customers
### Characteristics:
- Medium income
- Low to medium spending
- Moderate churn probability

### Business Strategy:
- Discount campaigns
- Seasonal promotions
- Bundle offers to increase engagement

---

## Segment 2: Young Professionals
### Characteristics:
- Young age group
- Moderate income
- High spending frequency
- High churn risk

### Business Strategy:
- Referral bonuses
- App-based personalized recommendations
- Social media promotions

---

## Conclusion
Customer segmentation helps identify valuable customer groups and enables targeted business strategies for improving retention and increasing revenue.
